<h1 id="k2l-guide">k2l <em>Guide</em></h1>
<p>Welcome to <strong>k2l</strong>, a <em>Kotlin</em> to Lua transpiler. See <a href="https://example.org/k2l">https://example.org/k2l</a> or <a href="https://example.org/docs">the docs</a>.
Use <code>x &lt; y &amp;&amp; y &gt; z</code> freely; it's <del>not</del> escaped.
